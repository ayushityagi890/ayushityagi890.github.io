# #1: Limber Women’s Fitness Studio

## 1. Brand Overview

Limber Women’s Fitness Studio is a women-only gym in Toronto. It
focuses on strength, flexibility, and wellness. They offer small
group classes like strength training, Pilates, and stretching.
The gym gives a friendly and safe environment for women to
exercise and feel confident.

## 2. Target Audience

Limber is for women of all fitness levels. Their members want a
comfortable and supportive space. Many women want personal
guidance, community, and a place where they feel safe compared
to big gyms.

## 3. Customer Needs / Problems

Women often feel uncomfortable in co-ed gyms. They want:

- Personal attention in classes
- Safety and comfort
- Programs that focus on women’s goals

Limber solves these problems by offering women-only classes,
small groups, and a friendly community.

## 4. Marketing Channels

Limber reaches customers through:

- Website – shows classes, location, and prices

- Instagram – shares tips, photos, and class
  highlights

- Reviews – members leave feedback on Google
  and other sites

## 5. Strengths

- Community and support – small classes make
  members feel noticed

- Women-only space – makes members feel
  confident

- Good reputation – many positive reviews
  online

## 6. Opportunities / 3 Ideas to Improve

- **Show member success stories** – Add short
  testimonials or pictures of members on the website and
  Instagram. This will help new people trust the gym and feel
  excited to join.
- **Post short workout videos** – Share 30–60
  second clips of exercises, tips, or class highlights on
  Instagram or TikTok. This can attract more followers and
  potential members.
- **Send weekly emails** – Send a simple newsletter
  with class schedules, fitness tips, and member stories. This
  keeps current members engaged and encourages them to bring
  friends.

## Summary

Limber is a strong, women-focused gym. It builds a loyal
community. By adding more digital content and member engagement,
it can attract even more women.

# #2 Limber Women’s Fitness Studio – Content & Community Strategy

## Context

Many women-only gyms focus on workouts, but they don’t always
focus on confidence, support, and community. Women stay longer
when they feel safe, motivated, and understood.

## Problem Identified

After initial curiosity, members stop coming. The reasons include:

- lack of motivation
- lack of accountability
- no feeling of progress
- no personal connection with the brand
- no sense of community

The gym needs more than marketing. It needs a community that women want to return to.

## Goal

Increase:

- Membership retention
- Member engagement
- Loyalty and referrals
- Brand love (soft metric)

## Target Audience

### Women ages 18 – 45 in Toronto who want:

- Safe space
- Women-only environment
- Supportive community
- Guidance and progress tracking
- No judgment from men

## Key Insight

Women are more consistent with fitness when:

- They feel supported
- They feel seen
- They feel part of something

Fitness becomes emotional, not just physical.

## Strategy Overview

My strategy was based on 3 pillars:

### 1. Content that Educates &amp; Empowers

Type of content:

- Beginner-friendly exercise tips
- Mindset and confidence messages
- Success stories from real members
- “Small wins” recognition posts
- Nutrition and habit guidance
- “Safe space” communication

Tone: friendly, simple language, no heavy
fitness jargon.

Example post themes:

- “5 no-equipment exercises for busy women”
- “Confidence is built, not born”
- “Small wins matter: Sara showed up 3 days this week”
- “How to start if you feel shy in the gym”

### 2. Community &amp; Accountability

- Weekly group classes
- Progress check-ins
- Women-only challenges
- Buddy system for new members
- Mental wellness workshops
- “Bring a friend” day

Fitness becomes social, not alone.

### 3. Retention &amp; Referrals

Retention tools:

- Milestone badges (consistency rewards)
- Referral bonuses
- Loyalty points
- Birthday offers
- Progress photos tracking (optional and private)
- Digital check-in streaks

Referrals are natural in supportive communities.

## Sample Campaign: “Confidence Month”

30-day campaign focused on confidence-building for women.
Weekly themes:

- Week 1: Showing Up
- Week 2: Learning the Basics
- Week 3: Feeling Strong
- Week 4: Celebrating Wins

Sample content pieces:

- Short videos with beginner modifications
- Testimonials from shy beginners
- Q&amp;A with female coaches
- Member-of-the-week highlight
- Open workshop for new joiners

## Expected Results

Clear measurable results expected:

- Higher class attendance
- Higher membership retention
- Increase in referrals
- Growth in Instagram engagement
- Stronger brand attachment

### Brand outcome:

Women don’t just go to the gym. They belong to the gym.
